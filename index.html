About

Dr. Watthanan Jatuviriyapornchai is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Mathematics, Faculty of Science, Mahidol University. Her research is centered on probability theory and stochastic models, with particular emphasis on interacting particle systems and the emergence of condensed phases in complex systems.

She received her PhD in Mathematics from the University of Warwick, UK. Her work combines rigorous analysis, stochastic modelling, and simulation-based approaches to understand collective behavior in systems with many interacting components.

Research Interests
  • Applied probability
  • Stochastic processes
  • Interacting particle systems
  • Condensation
  • Stochastic Monte Carlo methods
  • Mathematical modelling of complex systems
  • Interdisciplinary applications
Current Projects

  • Stochastic particle model of traffic flow

    Modelling traffic flow behavior for smart road development.

  • Voter modelling for elections

    Studying Thailand election dynamics through stochastic voter models.

  • Non-Markovian interacting particle systems

    Developing rigorous approaches for particle systems with memory.

  • Poisson-Dirichlet structures in condensation

    Investigating the role of Poisson-Dirichlet distributions in condensing particle systems.
