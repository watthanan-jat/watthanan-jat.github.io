SCIM311

Statistical Modelling

A course page for modelling data, checking assumptions, quantifying uncertainty, and communicating statistical evidence clearly.

Overview

SCIM311 develops the practical modelling workflow: understand the question, prepare the data, choose a statistical model, estimate parameters, check diagnostics, quantify uncertainty, and communicate conclusions.

Topics

Model Questions

Translate a real question into variables, assumptions, data requirements, and statistical hypotheses.

Fit Models

Use regression and related models to estimate relationships, compare explanations, and make predictions.

Check Assumptions

Use residual plots, diagnostics, transformations, and validation checks to understand model limitations.

Communicate Evidence

Report uncertainty, effect sizes, diagnostics, and practical interpretation in clear language.
